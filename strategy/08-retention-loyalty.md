## LOYALTY PROGRAM: "VAL INSIDER"

### Tier Structure (Hybrid: Points + Status)

| Tier | Requirement | Benefits | Perks |
|------|-------------|----------|-------|
| **Bronze** | 1st purchase | 1 pt/$ spent • VAL10 on renewal • Standard support | Welcome gift: Free activation guide PDF |
| **Silver** | 5 orders OR $150 LTV | 1.25 pt/$ • VAL15 (15% off) • Priority WhatsApp (<30 min) • Early access to new licenses | Monthly "Insider Picks" email • Birthday 20% off |
| **Gold** | 15 orders OR $500 LTV | 1.5 pt/$ • VAL20 (20% off) • VIP WhatsApp (<15 min) • Dedicated agent • Free renewals (1/year) | Quarterly strategy call • Co-branded referral link • Test new products free |

### Points System
- **Earn:** 1 point per $1 spent (multiplied by tier)
- **Redeem:** 100 points = $1 credit (min 500 pts = $5)
- **Expiry:** 12 months rolling (activity resets)
- **Bonus:** 500 pts for referral, 200 pts for review, 100 pts for activation confirmation

### Tier Progression Visibility
- **My Account page:** Progress bar, next tier benefits, points balance
- **Monthly email:** "You're 2 orders from Silver — unlock 15% off everything"
- **WhatsApp notification:** "🎉 You've reached Gold! 20% off for life + VIP support"

## REFERRAL PROGRAM: "VAL CIRCLE"

### Mechanics
- **Referrer:** $5 credit per successful referral (no limit)
- **Referee:** $5 off first order (min $15) + free guide
- **Tracking:** Unique referral link per customer (auto-generated)
- **Attribution:** 30-day cookie + WhatsApp deep link parameter
- **Payout:** Instant credit on referee's first successful delivery

### Referral Link Format
```
https://valshop.wasmer.app?ref=VAL-USER12345
https://wa.me/5491123456789?text=REF-VAL-USER12345
```

### Promotion Channels
- Post-purchase Day 3 (peak satisfaction)
- Monthly "Insider" email
- Account dashboard "Earn $5" card
- WhatsApp broadcast (monthly)
- Order confirmation page: "Share & earn $5"

### Viral Loop Enhancers
- **Milestone rewards:** 5 referrals = Silver tier free for 1 year
- **Leaderboard:** Monthly top referrers get Gold tier + $50 credit
- **Team referrals:** "Refer your design team — 5 seats = $25 each"

## REDUCING RETURNS / DISPUTES (Digital-Specific)

### Prevention
- **Pre-purchase:** Country compatibility checker on product page
- **At delivery:** Activation guide + "Test now" CTA
- **Post-delivery Day 1:** "Working? 👍/👎" → 👎 = instant replace

### Resolution Flow
```
Buyer: "Not working"
    ↓
Bot: "Send: Device + Error screenshot + Country" (template)
    ↓
Agent (auto-assigned): Diagnoses in 1 reply
    ↓
Fix works → "Great! Enjoy." → Tag resolved
Fix fails → "Replacing now" → New code in 2 min
    ↓
Still fails → "Full refund + keep the replacement" → Escalate to supplier audit
```

### Metrics
- **First-reply resolution:** Target >85%
- **Replacement rate:** Target <1.5%
- **Refund rate:** Target <0.5%
- **Chargeback rate:** Target <0.1%
