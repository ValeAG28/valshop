## SUPPORT PHILOSOPHY

**Core Principle:** Every support touchpoint is a trust-building moment. Speed + Accuracy + Humanity = Retention.

**Metrics That Matter:**
- First Response Time: **<2 hours** (Target: <30 min WhatsApp, <1h Email)
- First Contact Resolution: **>85%**
- CSAT: **>4.7/5**
- Support-Influenced Revenue: **Trackable via WhatsApp deep links**

## CRISIS PROTOCOLS

### Scenario: Mass Delivery Failure (Distributor API Down)
1. **Auto-Detect:** >5 "where is order" in 10 min → Alert Lead
2. **Comms:** Broadcast to affected: "Distributor delay. ETA 30 min. 20% credit auto-applied."
3. **Manual Override:** Senior agents use backup distributor pool
4. **Post-Mortem:** 24h → Root cause → Process fix → Customer email

### Scenario: Supplier Sends Bad Batch (Codes Revoked)
1. **Detect:** >3 "code revoked" in 1 hour → Alert Lead + Pause Supplier
2. **Comms:** Proactive email to all batch buyers: "Supplier issue. Replacing free. No action needed."
3. **Resolution:** Bulk replace from alternate supplier
4. **Compensation:** 1-month free extension + 20% credit

### Scenario: WhatsApp Business API Outage
1. **Fallback:** Email + Contact Form + Website Banner
2. **Communicate:** "WhatsApp temporary. Email support@valdigital.com — <1h reply."
3. **Queue:** All messages queued → Processed on restore
